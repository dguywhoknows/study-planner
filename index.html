A study schedule built around your exam dates and free hours.

Add your exams and how much time you have each day. Each syllabus is split into topics with time estimates, and the planner spreads them over your calendar with reviews before each exam. Missed a day? Replan from today.

Ask it to do things

Press Ctrl+K in the app (Cmd+K on a Mac) and describe what you want. The model chooses from Exam Planner's own functions, runs them in order and tells you what changed. Some examples:

Add a biology exam on the 20th covering cells, genetics and evolutionAdds the exam with its syllabus.
I can only study 2 hours on weekdaysUpdates your availability and rebuilds the plan.
Quiz me on my weakest topicOpens a practice quiz on the lowest-mastery topic.

Instructions you save in the same box, such as "keep it short" or "I'm a beginner", are added to every AI request the app makes.

Notes

Cost
Free. Each account has a daily allowance of AI requests.
Your data
What you make stays in this browser on this device. Your account is only used for signing in and for the built-in AI. You can export a backup or clear everything from Settings.
AI access
Signed-in users get the built-in AI with nothing to set up. You can also use your own Groq or OpenRouter key under Settings.
